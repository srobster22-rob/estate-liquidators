ESTATE LIQUIDATORS

Four nights to empty a dead collector's house before sunrise. The quota is in dollars, not items. Something in here is still curating the collection: it doesn't hunt intruders, it retrieves property — so it follows whatever is most valuable and currently leaving.

If your light dims, it is coming for you — and you can get rid of it by handing the vase to someone else.
